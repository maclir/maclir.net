Areh

areh.app ↗

A calendar invite works fine for organising a party, as long as everyone uses the same calendar. Nobody uses the same calendar.

Send one and see what happens. Some people are on Google and it lands perfectly. Some are on Apple and get an attachment. Some are signed into a work account on the laptop and a personal one on the phone, so the invite arrives somewhere they aren't. Half of them never press accept — not out of rudeness, but because accepting meant being logged into the right thing at the right moment.

So the answers come back through the group chat instead, one at a time, mixed in with everything else. You count them by scrolling. Meanwhile the address, the real start time and the thing about parking are forty messages up.

What it does

Areh is one page per event. You make it in about twenty seconds and share the link. Guests tap in or out. That is the whole thing — no account, no app, no calendar, no login.

You get a live count and a guest list. They get the details in one place that does not scroll away, and a notification if the plan changes.

It reads invitations too. Paste a link or a screenshot and it fills in the form, so you don't start by retyping a date out of someone else's message.

The name is Farsi. Areh means yeah, which is the whole app.
