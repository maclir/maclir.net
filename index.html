About

I build small things to fix problems I actually have.

A bill nobody wanted to split. A party nobody could RSVP to without an app. A shopping list written in the wrong order, in two languages. Each one started as something small that annoyed me, and stayed small enough for one person to finish.

None of them cost me anything to run when nobody is using them. That was a rule I set early on, and it turned out to be the part that makes them fun to build. It also means I have never had to switch one off to save eight euros a month.

Here is what each one does, and where it came from.
